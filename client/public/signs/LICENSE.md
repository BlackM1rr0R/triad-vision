# German traffic sign images — sources and licence

The SVG files in this folder show official German traffic signs (Verkehrszeichen) of the Straßenverkehrs-Ordnung
(StVO, Anlagen 1–3) and the Verkehrszeichenkatalog (VzKat). Official signs are *amtliche Werke* under
§ 5 Abs. 1 UrhG and therefore in the public domain in Germany. The drawings were downloaded once from
Wikimedia Commons (November 2026), where they are marked as public domain, sanitized (metadata, editor data
and any scripts or external references removed) and are served from this site; nothing is loaded from
Wikimedia at runtime.

`1002.svg` (Zusatzzeichen: Verlauf der Vorfahrtstraße) is a simplified drawing made for this site.

Legal texts: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/ (Anlage 1, 2 and 3).
